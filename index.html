Updated Financial & Sales Analysis (Data Fixed)

Sales & Costs (FY25 vs. FY26): Sales (Total Revenue) went down slightly from €26.30B to €25.86B (-1.68%), and Raw Material Costs also decreased from €21.33B to €20.66B (-3.15%).

Profitability Metrics (Chart 2): Operating Profit increased dramatically from €26,306B to €25,864B (+98.22%). Net Profit fell—from €1.50B to €1.40B (-6.48%).

Analysis — What This Means: Sales and Raw Material costs showed typical, minor year-over-year variance. However, the massive increase in Operating Profit indicates a significant one-off non-operational gain or a major data anomaly in the provided figures that dwarfs the scale of core operations and Net Profit.

Strategic Recommendations

  • 1. Boost Sales Volume: Support franchise sales to reverse the -1.68% dip.
  • 2. Vertical Integration: Use Baltic forestland acquisitions to keep input costs low.
  • 3. Investigate Operating Profit Anomaly: Audit the significant discrepancy driving the 98% OpProfit increase.
