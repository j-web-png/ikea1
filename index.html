Updated Financial & Sales Analysis (Corrected Figures)

Sales & Costs (FY25 vs. FY26): Sales (Total Revenue) went down slightly from €26.30B to €25.86B (-1.68%), and Raw Material Costs also decreased from €21.33B to €20.66B (-3.15%).

Profitability Metrics (Chart 2): Operating Profit decreased from €26.306B to €25.864B (-1.68%), mirroring the exact percentage decline in top-line sales. Net Profit fell more sharply—from €1.50B to €1.40B (-6.48%).

Analysis — What This Means: Core business performance weakened slightly in FY26. Since Operating Profit dropped by the same percentage as Sales, the company's fundamental operating margin remained stable but on a smaller revenue base. The larger Net Profit drop suggests that non-operational financial headwinds (like interest or currency impacts) had a disproportionately negative effect on the bottom line compared to core operations.

Strategic Recommendations

  • 1. Sales Growth Initiative: Implement targeted campaigns to reverse the -1.68% sales dip and grow the revenue base.
  • 2. Vertical Integration: Continue Baltic forestland acquisitions to secure supply and stabilize long-term input costs.
  • 3. Financial Hedging Review: Audit foreign exchange and financial risk management strategies to reduce volatility impacting net profit.
