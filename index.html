Strategic Priorities (FY26/FY27)

1. Margin Recovery & Cost Discipline: Immediate action is required to address the 15.22% increase in Other Operating Expenses. Implement stringent controls on discretionary spending and review indirect labor costs.

2. Optimize Raw Material Sourcing: Despite the cost decrease, procurement remains 80% of revenue. Explore near-shoring opportunities and renegotiate long-term contracts to further improve the material margin.

3. Restore Top-Line Growth: Investigate the 1.68% dip in wholesale revenue. Develop joint business plans with major franchisees to stimulate end-customer demand and optimize product availability.

4. Financial Hedging Strategy: Continue to leverage net financial income and currency gains to cushion the impact of lower operational profitability.
