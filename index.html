Simple Financial & Sales Analysis

What Happened to Sales & Costs (FY25 vs. FY26): Sales (Total Revenue) went down slightly from €26.30B to €25.86B (-1.68%), and Raw Material Costs also decreased from €21.33B to €20.66B (-3.15%).

Why Profit Dropped Less at the Bottom Line: Operating Profit (EBIT) dropped from €1.73B to €1.46B (-15.42%), lowering the operating margin from 6.56% to 5.64%. Net Profit fell less—from €1.50B to €1.40B (-6.48%)—because financial income and currency gains helped soften the drop.

Sales Analysis — What This Means: Sales dipped by €0.44B (-1.68%), which reduced the overall revenue base. Even though raw material costs decreased, the smaller sales base and other operating pressures meant core operating profit still fell. In short, demand/sales decreased slightly in FY26, so growing sales and protecting core margins matter more than relying on material cost drops alone.

Strategic Recommendations

  • 1. Boost Sales Volume: Support franchise sales and urban touchpoints to reverse the -1.68% dip.
  • 2. Vertical Integration: Use Baltic forestland acquisitions to keep input costs low.
  • 3. Protect Operating Margin: Keep core business costs disciplined so sales translate into EBIT.
