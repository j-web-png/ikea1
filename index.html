Core Business Analysis

The Situation: Total Revenue dipped slightly by 1.68% (€0.44B). Simultaneously, Other Operating Expenses (OpEx) jumped by 15.22% (€0.50B), largely absorbing savings from lower material costs.

The Impact: Operating Profit (EBIT) fell by over 15%, dropping from €1.73B to €1.46B. The operating margin compressed from 6.56% to 5.64%.

The Priority: To protect core operations, immediate focus is required on cost discipline regarding OpEx alongside strategies to restore top-line growth.
