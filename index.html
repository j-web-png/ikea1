Analysis & Split Graph Insights

Geographical Distribution: The geographical breakdown shows the contribution of the Netherlands, European Union and the rest of the world to total operating income. Comparing FY25 and FY26 highlights changes in regional distribution.

Operating Income: Separate graphs distinguish operating income from sales of goods from total operating income, allowing both measures to be compared independently.

Cost Structure: Raw materials and consumables are compared with other operating expenses and labour to illustrate changes in the cost structure between FY25 and FY26.

Worldwide Retail Sales: The horizontal bar chart provides a comparison of worldwide IKEA retail sales across the two financial years.

Profit Compression: Operating Profit (€1.46B) and Net Profit (€1.40B) demonstrate the difference between operating and net profitability.
