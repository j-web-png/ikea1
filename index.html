Plain-Language Financial Analysis

Revenue and Cost Categories (Chart 1): Between FY25 and FY26, Total Revenue dipped slightly from €26.30B to €25.86B (-1.68%), while Raw Material Costs also edged down from €21.33B to €20.66B (-3.15%). However, Other Operating Expenses (OpEx) rose from €3.25B to €3.75B (+15.22%), eating into the savings from lower material costs.

Profit & Margin Takeaway (Chart 2): Operating Profit (EBIT) fell from €1.73B to €1.46B (-15.42%), pulling the operating margin down from 6.56% to 5.64%. Net Profit declined less sharply—from €1.50B to €1.40B (-6.48%)—because financial items and currency gains helped soften the drop.

Strategic Recommendations

  • 1. Cap Non-Core OpEx: Set strict ceilings on operational expense growth.
  • 2. Vertical Integration: Use Baltic forestland acquisitions to control input costs.
  • 3. Franchise Expansion: Accelerate urban plan-and-order points to drive fee revenue.
